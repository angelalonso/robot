Phase 0 hardware schematic - provisional verification record
=============================================================

STATUS: NOT AUTHORITATIVE UNTIL THE PHASE 0 CHECKLIST IS SIGNED OFF.
Do not connect motor power while any polarity, ground, motor identity, or
GPIO direction remains unknown.

POWER PATHS
-----------

Pi power bank                         Motor battery pack
  +5V ---------------------> Pi 5V     +V -- fuse -- switch --> L298N VMOT
  GND ----------------------+           0V -------------------+------------+
                            |                                |            |
                         Pi GND                         L298N GND    (Arduino
                                                                    GND only
                                                                    if used)

The Pi power bank and motor battery are intended to be separate power sources.
The L298N 5 V regulator/jumper state is UNKNOWN until measured. Do not use the
L298N regulator to power the Pi without an electrical design review.

RASPBERRY PI TO L298N
---------------------

Function                 BCM GPIO    Physical header pin    L298N terminal
Left motor IN1           27          13                     IN1
Left motor IN2           17          11                     IN2
Left motor enable/PWM    22          15                     ENA
Right motor IN1          24          18                     IN1
Right motor IN2          23          16                     IN2
Right motor enable/PWM   25          22                     ENB
Logic ground             --          GND                    GND

The right-motor IN1/IN2 order conflicts with historical chassis notes. The
template currently uses IN1=GPIO24 and IN2=GPIO23. Verify continuity and
direction with wheels lifted before applying normal motor power.

L298N MOTOR OUTPUTS
-------------------

L298N OUT1/OUT2  ------> physically labelled LEFT MOTOR (UNVERIFIED)
L298N OUT3/OUT4  ------> physically labelled RIGHT MOTOR (UNVERIFIED)

Do not infer left/right from OUT1/OUT2 or OUT3/OUT4 labels. Record the actual
mapping after inspection.

STATUS LED
----------

Pi BCM GPIO21 / physical pin 40 ----> current-limiting resistor (VALUE UNKNOWN)
                                      ----> LED ----> GND

The resistor value, LED polarity, and physical connection must be verified.

TEST POINTS
-----------

TP1: motor battery positive to motor battery ground, no load
TP2: Pi 5V input to Pi ground
TP3: Pi ground to L298N logic ground continuity
TP4: each GPIO wire to its labelled L298N terminal
TP5: motor battery positive to every Pi GPIO (must NOT conduct)
